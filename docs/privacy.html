Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Cubolume works on your device. We do not collect your sounds, patterns, recordings, or personal information through the app. No account is required.

Who we are

Cubolume is developed by Martin Adler. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Cubolume iPhone and iPad app, its AUv3 extension ("Cubolume: Sonic Cube"), and its support website.

Information handled by the app

Sound playback, sequencing, and audio rendering run entirely on your device. Cubolume does not upload your patches, recordings, or performances to an AI service or developer-operated server.

The app does not include advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

Microphone

If you choose Record microphone inside Samples, Cubolume records audio only while you are actively using that feature, after you grant microphone permission, to create a sound you install on the cube. Recordings stay on your device. Cubolume does not transmit microphone audio anywhere.

Local storage and backups

Your cube layout, sound assignments, sequencer patterns, mixer and FX settings, imported/recorded samples, and saved patches are stored in the app's storage on your device. We cannot access this information remotely.

Depending on your device settings, Apple may include app data in device backups or transfers. Cubolume does not operate its own cloud storage or synchronization service. Apple's handling of backups is governed by your Apple settings and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Exports and portable patches

Render and share WAV, and Share patch + samples, create files on your device. You choose their destination through the system share sheet or Files; a destination may be local storage or a cloud provider you have configured. Cubolume does not upload these files to its own servers.

AUv3 hosts

When you use the Cubolume AUv3 extension inside a host app (such as GarageBand or AUM), the host can save the extension's patch and settings in its own project and receive generated audio or MIDI from Cubolume. The host app and any storage or sharing services you choose handle that information according to their own settings and privacy practices. Removing Cubolume does not remove copies saved in host projects or exported files.

Support correspondence

If you email martoadler@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and provide support. Please avoid including sensitive information that is not needed for your question.

Support correspondence may be retained while needed to handle the request and maintain relevant support records. You can request deletion by contacting the same address, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell your information or share app usage data with advertisers or data brokers. Your locally stored sounds and patches are not collected by us. Support emails are processed by the email services used to deliver and answer them.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete your recordings, samples, and patches at any time from within the app. Uninstalling Cubolume removes its local app data from your device, subject to how your device handles app data and backups.

Children's privacy

Cubolume is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Website hosting

This support and privacy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web server logs (such as IP address and browser type) according to GitHub's Privacy Statement. We do not add separate analytics or tracking to this site.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact
